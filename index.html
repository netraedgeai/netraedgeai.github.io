FAQ

Common questions

 

Can I get a custom project made?

Yes. Describe it in the order form. We tell you if it is possible, the cost and the time.

Do you make projects for school and college?

Yes. We build working models and explain how they work so you can present them.

Will it work without internet?

Many products also work with manual switches or on the local network. Tell us your need and we plan it that way.

How do I pay and when will I get it?

We discuss price, advance and delivery time on call or WhatsApp after you send the order. Nothing is charged on this website.

Which boards do you use?

Raspberry Pi, ESP32, ESP32-CAM, ESP32-S3 and Arduino. We pick the best one for your job.
